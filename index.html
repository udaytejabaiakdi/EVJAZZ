Battery Technology Deep Dive

Graphene vs Lithium: Which Battery Should You Buy?

Every EV Jazz model is available with either cost-effective Graphene (Lead-Carbon) or lightweight high-performance Lithium-Ion. Here is the honest comparison of price, weight, charging, and long-term value.

Graphene Setup (60V System)
🔋🔋🔋🔋🔋
5 Batteries = ~38 to 40 kg

Requires 5 separate 12V modules in series to produce 60V. Fixed non-detachable setup inside chassis.

VS
Lithium-Ion Pack (60V System)
⚡1 Single Smart Pack
1 Battery = ~8.5 to 9.5 kg

Saves ~30 kg of dead weight! 100% detachable — carry upstairs like a laptop bag.

Affordability Champion • Low Initial Cost

Graphene (Lead-Carbon)

Standard high-density sealed lead-carbon series bank (60V 32AH)

🔋 5x Heavy 12V Lead-Carbon Batteries (~38–40 kg)
🔒 NON-REMOVABLE (Fixed Inside Scooter)
Affordability Very High (Lowest Cost)
Portability Fixed / Not Removable
Charging Speed 6 to 8 Hours (Slow)
Total Weight ~38 – 40 kg (5 Units)
🔋 Battery Pack Count 5 Batteries (12V x 5 in Series)
🔌 Removable / Portable? ❌ NOT Removable (Fixed Inside Chassis)
⚖️ Total Weight Penalty ~38 to 40 kg (+30 kg Heavier)
⏳ Charging Duration 6 to 8 Hours (Standard Overnight)
🔥 Driving Heat Status Higher Internal Resistance / Warms on Throttle
🔄 Cycle Life & Longevity 400 – 500 Cycles (~1.5 to 2 Years)
🏷️ Starting Scooter Price ₹42,999 to ₹46,000 (Maximum Savings)
✅ Why Choose Graphene? (High Affordability at Low Cost)

Maximum Upfront Affordability: The affordability of Graphene is exceptionally high at low cost! It gets you on the road with a brand new EV scooter starting at just ₹42,999. If you have ground floor parking with a charging socket and short daily rides (15-25 km), Graphene offers the highest initial cost savings.

⚠️ Why NOT Graphene? (Not Removable & Heavy)

Graphene is NOT Removable: The 5 heavy batteries are permanently installed inside the scooter frame. You cannot take them upstairs to charge inside your flat or home. Adds ~40 kg of dead weight, takes 6-8 hours to charge, and has a shorter 1.5-2 year replacement cycle.

Removable • Fast 3-4h Charge • Zero Heating

Lithium-Ion (Smart Pack)

High energy density smart battery pack with intelligent BMS (60V 27AH / 32AH / 41AH)

⚡ 1x Smart Removable Lithium Pack with Carry Handle (~8.5–9.5 kg)
✨ 100% REMOVABLE (Carry Home & Charge Anywhere)
Portability 100% Removable Carry Pack
Charging Speed Fast in 3 to 4 Hours
Thermal Control NO Heating While Driving
Weight Savings Only ~9 kg (-30 kg Saved!)
🔋 Battery Pack Count 1 Smart Pack (Replaces all 5 Graphenes!)
🔌 Removable / Portable? ✅ 100% REMOVABLE (Built-in Carry Handle)
⚖️ Total Weight ~8.5 to 9.5 kg (75% Lighter · 30 kg Saved)
⏳ Charging Duration ⚡ Fast in 3 to 4 Hours (2x Faster)
❄️ Driving Heat Status ❄️ NO Heating While Driving (Smart BMS)
🔄 Cycle Life & Longevity 1,500 – 2,000 Cycles (4 to 6+ Years)
🏷️ Starting Scooter Price ₹62,000 to ₹74,000 (Best Long-Term ROI)
⭐ Carry & Charge at Home with Removable Battery

Lithium is 100% Removable: Never worry about having a ground-floor plug or parking socket. Simply lift the seat, unplug the heavy-duty safety socket, and carry the sleek ~9 kg battery right into your home, apartment flat, or office to charge at any 5A wall socket!

⚡ Faster Charging in 3 to 4 Hours & Zero Heating

Faster Charging & No Heating While Driving: Lithium recharges in just 3 to 4 hours (half the time of graphene). Furthermore, high-grade cells and an intelligent BMS ensure zero battery heating while driving, delivering consistent punch on steep inclines and flyovers even in peak summer heat.

🏢
Must Choose Lithium

Apartment & Flat Dwellers

If you live on an upper floor without dedicated parking charging sockets, Lithium is mandatory. The 9 kg pack carries easily upstairs like luggage.

💵
Best for Budget: Graphene

Tight Budget & Short Commute

If upfront price is your #1 constraint and you have a ground floor garage socket for daily 15-25 km local market trips, Graphene starting at ₹42,999 saves you ₹20,000+ initially.

🚀
Best Long-Term: Lithium

Daily Commuters & Deliveries

If you ride 40-70 km daily or want 5+ years of peace of mind with quick top-up charges and spirited acceleration, Lithium pays for itself with 3x longer lifespan and zero battery fatigue.
